Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026  |  Applies to scaleupcowork.com operated by ScaleUp CoWork, Pune

ScaleUp CoWork (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to safeguarding the personal information you share when visiting https://scaleupcowork.com, submitting tour requests, requesting workspace quotes, or contacting our team across our Pune centres.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a tour request, claim a Free 1-Day Trial Pass, request pricing, or reach out via WhatsApp/phone, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, phone / WhatsApp number, email address, and optional company or organization name.
  • Workspace Requirements: Selected centre (such as Deccan Hub Flagship or Law College Road Centre), workspace plan preference (Day Pass, Dedicated Desk, Hybrid Cowork, Private Cabin, Meeting Room, or Virtual Office), team seat count, preferred visit date, and optional inquiry notes.
  • Technical & Usage Signals: Non-personally identifiable analytics data such as browser type, device viewport, referring URL, and pages viewed to maintain site reliability and measure advertising performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly for legitimate business and customer-service purposes:

  • Scheduling your centre walkthrough, reserving a Free 1-Day Trial Pass, or preparing a customized office seat/cabin quotation.
  • Contacting you by phone call, WhatsApp, or email in direct response to your submitted inquiry.
  • Maintaining internal CRM inquiry logs and managing ongoing workspace memberships.
  • Improving website navigation, page speed, and relevance of our Google Ads campaigns.

3. Cookies, Google Tag Manager & Google Ads Tracking

scaleupcowork.com uses Google Tag Manager (GTM-5FD2T97P), Google Analytics, and Google Ads conversion and remarketing tags/cookies. These technologies help us understand how visitors interact with our pages, measure which advertisements generate tour inquiries, and display relevant ScaleUp CoWork offers across Google Search and partner sites.

How to Opt Out or Control Cookies:

  • You can manage ad personalization and opt out of Google’s use of cookies for personalized advertising anytime at Google Ad Settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • You may also disable or delete cookies through your web browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) at any time, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

Lead submissions from our website forms are securely transmitted and recorded using Google Workspace / Google Sheets webhooks so our community managers can follow up promptly. When you choose to click a WhatsApp button, your pre-filled inquiry opens directly in the WhatsApp Business API / WhatsApp application with our official number (+91 98222 68333).

Zero Data Resale Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party telemarketers, brokers, or external mailing lists.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain inquiry and tour booking records only as long as needed to respond to your workspace request, maintain client records, or satisfy applicable legal/tax compliance obligations (such as Virtual Office KYC documentation). Form submissions may also be backed up locally in your browser storage (scaleup_leads_log) to prevent accidental form-submission loss. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your inquiry data at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer below.
